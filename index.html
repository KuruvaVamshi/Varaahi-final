Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about investing in a Vaaraahi property.

Are all Vaaraahi projects RERA registered and approved by banks?

Yes, 100%. We never launch or accept bookings without full statutory clearances from RERA and local urban development authorities. Additionally, all our developments have Approved Project Finance (APF) with premier national banks including SBI, HDFC, and ICICI.

Do you provide dedicated concierge services for NRI buyers?

Over 40% of our luxury clientele are Non-Resident Indians across the US, UK, and GCC. We offer an end-to-end digital investment process including 4K live video walk-throughs, Power of Attorney assistance, digital KYC, and remote bank loan facilitation.

What is the 300-Point Zero-Snag Guarantee?

Before any keys are handed over, our senior quality engineering team conducts a 300-point physical inspection covering thermal imaging for waterproofing, laser level checks on marble, electrical safety load tests, and acoustic door sealing to ensure you move into perfection.
